1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs mileage reimbursement for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to budget owners and finance personnel. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Approved business mileage is reimbursed at LKR 85 per kilometre.

3. Operating Procedure
Trip logs must state purpose, route, and distance. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Home-to-primary-office commuting is not reimbursable. Questions about interpretation are referred to Finance Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The Finance Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

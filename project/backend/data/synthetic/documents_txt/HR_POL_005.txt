1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs overtime authorisation for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Overtime must be approved in writing before work begins.

3. Operating Procedure
The standard weekly overtime ceiling is 8 hours. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency maintenance may be approved retrospectively within 1 business day. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs sick leave reporting for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Employees receive 12 paid sick leave days in each calendar year.

3. Operating Procedure
Absences must be reported to the line manager before 10:00 local time. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive workdays. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

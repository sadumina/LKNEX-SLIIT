1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs month-end financial close for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to budget owners and finance personnel. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Expense submissions for month-end close are due by the third business day of the next month.

3. Operating Procedure
Finance publishes the provisional close by the seventh business day. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Late material adjustments require controller approval. Questions about interpretation are referred to Finance Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The Finance Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

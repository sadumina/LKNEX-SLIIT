1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs document version control for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to policy owners and business unit leads. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Approved policy documents use semantic version numbers and a named owner.

3. Operating Procedure
Superseded versions must be archived and marked inactive. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency amendments require a recorded approval trail. Questions about interpretation are referred to Governance Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Governance Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

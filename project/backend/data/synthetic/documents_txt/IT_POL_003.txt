1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs security incident reporting for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff and managed service operators. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Suspected security incidents must be reported to the service desk within 30 minutes of discovery.

3. Operating Procedure
The security team assigns a severity category after triage. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Employees must not delete relevant logs or evidence. Questions about interpretation are referred to Information Security.

5. Compliance and Records
The Information Security team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs privileged access management for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff and managed service operators. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Privileged access is granted for a maximum of 24 hours per approved request.

3. Operating Procedure
Approvals require a ticket and a second authorised reviewer. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency access is logged and reviewed the next business day. Questions about interpretation are referred to Information Security.

5. Compliance and Records
The Information Security team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

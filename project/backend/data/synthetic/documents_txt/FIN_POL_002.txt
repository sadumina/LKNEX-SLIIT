1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs invoice processing timelines for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to budget owners and finance personnel. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Valid supplier invoices are scheduled for payment within 30 calendar days of acceptance.

3. Operating Procedure
Invoice matching requires purchase order and goods receipt references. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Disputed invoices remain on hold until resolved. Questions about interpretation are referred to Finance Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The Finance Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

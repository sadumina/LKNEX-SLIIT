1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs domestic business travel for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Domestic hotel reimbursement is capped at LKR 18000 per night.

3. Operating Procedure
Bookings require department-head approval before purchase. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
An exception requires written finance approval and an itemised invoice. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

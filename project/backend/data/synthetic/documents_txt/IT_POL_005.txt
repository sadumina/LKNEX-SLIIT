1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs backup and restore operations for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff and managed service operators. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Tier-one databases are backed up every 6 hours.

3. Operating Procedure
Restore tests are performed at least once every quarter. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Backup copies must be encrypted at rest and in transit. Questions about interpretation are referred to Information Security.

5. Compliance and Records
The Information Security team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

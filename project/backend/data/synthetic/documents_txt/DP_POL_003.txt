1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs personal data request handling for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff handling organisational information. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Privacy requests must be acknowledged within 5 business days.

3. Operating Procedure
Identity must be verified before disclosing account-specific data. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Complex requests may be escalated to the privacy officer. Questions about interpretation are referred to Privacy Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Privacy Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

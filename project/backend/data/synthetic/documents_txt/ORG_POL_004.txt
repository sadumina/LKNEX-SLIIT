1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs quality review checklist for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to policy owners and business unit leads. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Customer-facing process changes require a peer review before release.

3. Operating Procedure
Review records must identify findings and remediation owners. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Editorial-only corrections may use an expedited review. Questions about interpretation are referred to Governance Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Governance Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

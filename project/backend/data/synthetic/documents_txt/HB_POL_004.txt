1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs company equipment custody for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Assigned laptops must be returned within 3 business days after separation.

3. Operating Procedure
Asset transfers require a signed handover record. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Lost devices must be reported to IT within 1 hour of discovery. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

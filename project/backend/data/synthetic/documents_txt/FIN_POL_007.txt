1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs fixed asset capitalisation for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to budget owners and finance personnel. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Equipment purchases of LKR 150000 or more are capitalised.

3. Operating Procedure
Assets must receive a unique register identifier. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Consumable supplies are expensed regardless of total order size. Questions about interpretation are referred to Finance Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The Finance Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

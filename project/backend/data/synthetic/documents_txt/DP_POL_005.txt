1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs document redaction procedure for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff handling organisational information. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Personal identifiers must be masked before documents enter a public knowledge base.

3. Operating Procedure
A second reviewer checks high-risk redactions. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Original documents remain in the restricted repository. Questions about interpretation are referred to Privacy Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Privacy Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs enterprise risk register for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to policy owners and business unit leads. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Risk owners review HIGH risks at least every 30 days.

3. Operating Procedure
CRITICAL risks must be escalated within 1 business day. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Risk closure requires evidence of implemented mitigation. Questions about interpretation are referred to Governance Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Governance Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs business continuity exercise for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to policy owners and business unit leads. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Each business unit must conduct one continuity exercise every 12 months.

3. Operating Procedure
Exercise outcomes are documented within 10 business days. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Critical services require an identified recovery owner. Questions about interpretation are referred to Governance Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Governance Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs employee grievance resolution for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Human Resources must acknowledge a formal grievance within 3 business days.

3. Operating Procedure
A case manager should provide an initial response within 10 business days. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Anonymous submissions are reviewed but cannot receive personal case updates. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

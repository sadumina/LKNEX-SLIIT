1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs information classification standard for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff handling organisational information. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Company information is classified as PUBLIC, INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL, or RESTRICTED.

3. Operating Procedure
RESTRICTED records may be accessed only by explicitly authorised roles. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Classification labels must be preserved in exports and backups. Questions about interpretation are referred to Privacy Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Privacy Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs department budget variance for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to budget owners and finance personnel. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
A forecast variance exceeding 10 percent requires a written explanation.

3. Operating Procedure
Variance reviews occur monthly. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Approved capital projects follow separate reporting rules. Questions about interpretation are referred to Finance Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The Finance Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

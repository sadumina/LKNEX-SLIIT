1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs third-party data sharing for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff handling organisational information. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Confidential data transfers require an approved data-sharing agreement.

3. Operating Procedure
Transfer logs must include recipient, purpose, and approval reference. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Public marketing materials are outside the confidential-transfer workflow. Questions about interpretation are referred to Privacy Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Privacy Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

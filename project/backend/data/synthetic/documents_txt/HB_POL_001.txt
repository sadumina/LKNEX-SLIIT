1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs workplace code of conduct for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Employees must disclose a potential conflict of interest within 5 business days of discovery.

3. Operating Procedure
The disclosure is submitted through the internal ethics portal. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
A disclosure does not itself establish misconduct. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

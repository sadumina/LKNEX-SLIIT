1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs annual leave entitlement for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Full-time staff receive 21 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

3. Operating Procedure
Applies to employees after their first 90 days of employment. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Unused leave may carry forward up to 5 days until 31 March of the following year. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

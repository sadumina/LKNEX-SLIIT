1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs professional development allowance for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Eligible staff may claim up to LKR 45000 per fiscal year for approved training.

3. Operating Procedure
Requests must be submitted at least 10 business days before enrolment. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Travel and accommodation are excluded from the allowance. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

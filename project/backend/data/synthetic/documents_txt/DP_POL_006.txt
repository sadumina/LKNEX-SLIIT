1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs secure data disposal for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff handling organisational information. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Approved deletion requests must be executed within 20 business days.

3. Operating Procedure
Deletion jobs must produce an auditable completion record. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Legal holds suspend deletion until formally released. Questions about interpretation are referred to Privacy Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Privacy Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

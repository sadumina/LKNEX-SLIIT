1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs governance meeting records for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to policy owners and business unit leads. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Formal committee minutes must be published internally within 5 business days.

3. Operating Procedure
Minutes must record attendees, decisions, and action owners. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Restricted agenda items are stored in a limited-access annex. Questions about interpretation are referred to Governance Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Governance Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

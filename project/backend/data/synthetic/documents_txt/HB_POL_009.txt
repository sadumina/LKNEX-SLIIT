1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs internal communication standards for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Material operational announcements must be posted to the staff portal.

3. Operating Procedure
Urgent safety alerts must also use the designated emergency channel. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Personal messaging accounts must not be used to share restricted documents. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

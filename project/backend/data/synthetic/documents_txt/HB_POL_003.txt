1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs new employee onboarding for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
New employees must complete mandatory orientation within 10 business days.

3. Operating Procedure
The orientation includes safety, privacy, and acceptable-use modules. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Contractors receive a role-specific induction instead. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

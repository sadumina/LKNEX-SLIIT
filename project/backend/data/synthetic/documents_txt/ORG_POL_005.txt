1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs supplier performance reviews for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to policy owners and business unit leads. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Strategic suppliers are reviewed every 6 months.

3. Operating Procedure
Reviews consider delivery, quality, and security obligations. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Poor performance triggers a documented corrective action plan. Questions about interpretation are referred to Governance Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Governance Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs software change management for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff and managed service operators. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Production changes require a recorded change request at least 2 business days in advance.

3. Operating Procedure
A rollback plan and reviewer approval are mandatory. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Emergency fixes may follow the expedited change procedure. Questions about interpretation are referred to Information Security.

5. Compliance and Records
The Information Security team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

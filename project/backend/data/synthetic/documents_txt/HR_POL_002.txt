1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs probation and confirmation for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
The standard probation period is 6 months from the employment start date.

3. Operating Procedure
Applies to newly hired permanent staff. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
A documented extension may not exceed 3 additional months. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

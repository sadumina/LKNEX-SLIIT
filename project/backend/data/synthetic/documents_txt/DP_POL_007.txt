1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs data access audit logging for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff handling organisational information. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Access to RESTRICTED documents must be logged with user, timestamp, and document ID.

3. Operating Procedure
Audit logs are retained for 365 days. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Log alteration is limited to the security audit service. Questions about interpretation are referred to Privacy Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Privacy Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

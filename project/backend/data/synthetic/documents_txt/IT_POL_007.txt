1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs remote network access for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff and managed service operators. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Corporate VPN sessions expire after 12 hours.

3. Operating Procedure
VPN access requires a managed device and multifactor authentication. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Connections from blocked geographic regions require security approval. Questions about interpretation are referred to Information Security.

5. Compliance and Records
The Information Security team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

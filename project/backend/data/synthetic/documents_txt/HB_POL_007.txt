1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs employee recognition programme for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Peer recognition nominations close on the 20th day of each month.

3. Operating Procedure
A panel reviews nominations during the following month. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Panel members must recuse themselves from conflicts of interest. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

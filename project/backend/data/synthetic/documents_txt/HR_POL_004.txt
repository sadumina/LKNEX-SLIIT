1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs hybrid work arrangement for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees and people managers. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week.

3. Operating Procedure
Eligibility requires 60 days of completed service and manager approval. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
On-site attendance is mandatory for designated customer-facing shifts. Questions about interpretation are referred to People Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The People Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs procurement approval thresholds (superseded edition) for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to budget owners and finance personnel. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Purchases above LKR 200000 require two independent approvals.

3. Operating Procedure
Purchases above LKR 1000000 also require procurement committee review. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Splitting purchases to avoid thresholds is prohibited. Questions about interpretation are referred to Finance Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The Finance Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

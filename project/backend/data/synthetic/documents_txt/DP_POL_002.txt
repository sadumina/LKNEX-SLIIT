1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs records retention schedule for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff handling organisational information. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Routine support tickets are retained for 18 months after closure.

3. Operating Procedure
Records under legal hold must not be deleted. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Retention exceptions require approval from the records officer. Questions about interpretation are referred to Privacy Office.

5. Compliance and Records
The Privacy Office team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

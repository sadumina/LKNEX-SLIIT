1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs endpoint security baseline for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff and managed service operators. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Managed endpoints must install critical security patches within 7 calendar days.

3. Operating Procedure
Full-disk encryption is required for portable company devices. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Unsupported operating systems are blocked from corporate VPN access. Questions about interpretation are referred to Information Security.

5. Compliance and Records
The Information Security team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

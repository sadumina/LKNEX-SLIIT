1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs vendor due diligence for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to budget owners and finance personnel. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
New vendors must pass screening before their first payment.

3. Operating Procedure
Vendor bank-account changes require independent callback verification. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Low-value one-time vendors still require sanctions screening. Questions about interpretation are referred to Finance Operations.

5. Compliance and Records
The Finance Operations team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs password and authentication standard for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to staff and managed service operators. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
User passwords must contain at least 14 characters.

3. Operating Procedure
Multifactor authentication is mandatory for all administrative accounts. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Shared accounts are prohibited except approved service identities. Questions about interpretation are referred to Information Security.

5. Compliance and Records
The Information Security team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs employee expense submission (superseded edition) for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Routine expense claims must be submitted within 10 calendar days of purchase.

3. Operating Procedure
Each claim must include an itemised receipt. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Claims older than 15 days require finance-manager justification. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

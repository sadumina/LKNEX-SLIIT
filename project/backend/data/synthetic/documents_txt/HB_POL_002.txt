1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs attendance and time recording for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
Employees must record start and end times on each scheduled workday.

3. Operating Procedure
Corrections must be requested within 2 business days. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Approved field assignments may use the mobile attendance form. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.

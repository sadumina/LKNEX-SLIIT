1. Purpose and Applicability
This fictional policy governs office visitor registration for the Aster Vale Services simulation. It applies to employees, contractors and visitors where applicable. This training document is not a policy of a real organisation.

2. Core Requirement
All visitors must register at reception and display a visitor badge.

3. Operating Procedure
Hosts must accompany visitors in restricted work areas. The responsible owner must keep a dated approval record and communicate material changes through the internal policy portal.

4. Exceptions and Escalation
Delivery personnel may access only the designated loading zone. Questions about interpretation are referred to Workplace Experience.

5. Compliance and Records
The Workplace Experience team maintains compliance evidence, including review decisions and exception logs. Records are reviewed during scheduled internal assurance checks.
